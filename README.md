# **The Wild Robot**

*A beautiful story about survival, friendship, and finding your place in the world.*

---

## **Story Summary**

After a shipwreck, an intelligent robot named **Roz** becomes stranded on an uninhabited island.  
To survive the harsh surroundings, she:

- Learns to understand the local animals  
- Adapts to the natural environment  
- Cares for an orphaned baby goose  

> *“A robot can learn anything… even how to love.”*

---

## **Main Characters**

### **Roz**
The central robot character who grows emotionally throughout the story.

### **Brightbill**
The orphaned gosling Roz adopts and raises as her own.

---

## **Themes**

1. **Survival**
2. **Family and Belonging**
3. **Technology vs. Nature**

---

## **Why I Like This Movie**

- **Warm and emotional storytelling**  
- **Strong character development**  
- **Beautiful mix of technology and nature**
